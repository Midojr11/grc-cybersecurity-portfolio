# GRC & Cybersecurity Portfolio

## About Me

I am a cybersecurity and business administration graduate pursuing opportunities in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), IT risk, and cybersecurity compliance.

My background combines cybersecurity knowledge with an understanding of business operations, allowing me to approach security from both a technical and business-risk perspective. I hold multiple industry certifications, including CompTIA Security+, CySA+, PenTest+, Network+, A+, ISC2 SSCP, and ITIL 4 Foundation.

This portfolio demonstrates my practical application of risk assessment, security controls, policy development, remediation planning, and cybersecurity frameworks.

---

## GRC Portfolio Project — Mido Technologies

Mido Technologies is a fictional small-business environment originally developed as part of my WGU cybersecurity capstone.

The project began with an assessment of security weaknesses and remediation strategies. I expanded the original work into a GRC-focused portfolio demonstrating risk management, control mapping, policy development, and executive-level security communication.

### Risk Register

Identified and documented cybersecurity risks involving wireless security, network segmentation, identity and access management, patch management, endpoint security, backups, email authentication, and security awareness.

The register includes:

- Likelihood and impact assessments
- Inherent risk ratings
- Risk treatment strategies
- Security controls
- Risk ownership
- Residual risk
- Remediation status

**Artifact:** `Mido_Technologies_GRC_Risk_Register.xlsx`

---

### NIST CSF 2.0 Control Mapping

Mapped identified risks and remediation activities to relevant NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 functions and categories.

Areas include:

- Identity Management and Access Control
- Awareness and Training
- Platform Security
- Technology Infrastructure Resilience
- Data Security
- Continuous Monitoring

**Artifact:** `Mido_Technologies_NIST_CSF_2.0_Control_Mapping.xlsx`

---

### Executive Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

Translated technical cybersecurity findings into an executive-level risk assessment focused on business impact, remediation priorities, residual exposure, and management recommendations.

**Artifact:** `Mido_Technologies_Executive_Risk_Assessment.pdf`

### Access Control Policy

Developed an access control policy addressing:

- Unique user accounts
- Password requirements
- Multi-factor authentication
- Privileged access
- Least privilege
- Account lifecycle management
- Access reviews
- Compensating controls for legacy systems
- Network access restrictions

**Artifact:** `Mido_Technologies_Access_Control_Policy.pdf`

---

### Incident Response Plan

Developed a structured incident response process covering:

1. Preparation
2. Detection and Analysis
3. Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Post-Incident Review

The plan also establishes incident severity classifications, responsibilities, documentation requirements, and testing expectations.

**Artifact:** `Mido_Technologies_Incident_Response_Plan.pdf`
